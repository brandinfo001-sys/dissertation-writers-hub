Introduction

This Cookie Policy provides general information about cookies and similar technologies that may be associated with the Dissertation Writers Hub website.

The exact technologies used by a website can depend on its hosting environment, embedded resources and any analytics, advertising or other third-party services that are actually configured.

This policy should therefore be updated if new website tools or services are introduced that materially change how browser information is stored or accessed.

02

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website or related service can store on a user's device through a web browser.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as remembering preferences, maintaining website functionality, understanding how a website is used or supporting third-party features.

Similar technologies can include browser storage, pixels or other mechanisms that store or access information on a device.

03

Common Types of Cookies

Websites may use different categories of cookies depending on their functionality and the services that have been configured.

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These support functionality required for a website or service to operate properly.

Preference or Functional Cookies

These may remember choices or settings made by a user to provide a more convenient browsing experience.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics technologies may collect information about website usage so that website owners can better understand performance and visitor behaviour.

Advertising or Marketing Cookies

Some websites use advertising technologies to measure marketing activity or provide personalised advertising.

Important

The categories above describe common web technologies generally. They should not be interpreted as confirmation that every category is currently used by Dissertation Writers Hub.

04

Current Website Use

The current Dissertation Writers Hub website is designed as a static website and does not claim to use analytics, advertising or behavioural tracking services unless such technologies are actually configured.

The website does use external web resources and links, which may involve communication with third-party services when those resources are loaded or when a user chooses to follow an external link.

If analytics, advertising, consent-management tools or additional embedded services are added later, this Cookie Policy should be reviewed and updated accordingly.

No Invented Tracking Claims

This policy does not state that Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising cookies or other tracking systems are active unless those services are actually added to the website.

05

Essential Website Technologies

Some technical processing may be necessary for a website to be delivered securely and correctly through its hosting environment.

This may include standard technical requests, security functions, content delivery or other processes required to make website pages and resources available.

Whether those processes use cookies depends on the actual hosting and services configured for the live website.

06

Analytics Technologies

Analytics tools can be used by websites to understand information such as page visits, device types, navigation behaviour and general website performance.

Dissertation Writers Hub should only describe a specific analytics provider in this policy if that provider has actually been configured on the live website.

If analytics functionality is introduced later, the Cookie Policy and any required consent controls should be reviewed before or at the time that functionality becomes active.

07

  • WhatsApp.

Dissertation Writers Hub does not control the cookie practices of independent third-party platforms.

Third-Party Policies

You should review the cookie and privacy information provided by an external platform if you want to understand how that platform handles browser or device information.

08

External Website Resources

The website may load resources from external providers where those resources are required for website presentation or functionality.

For example, website fonts or similar resources may be requested from an external provider when a page loads.

The way an external provider processes technical information is governed by that provider's own policies.

If additional embedded tools, videos, maps, widgets or other third-party resources are introduced later, their cookie implications should be reviewed before publication.

09

Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers provide controls that allow users to review, delete or restrict cookies.

Browser settings may allow you to:

  • View stored cookies.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block cookies from particular websites.
  • Block some or all third-party cookies.
  • Configure how new cookies are handled.
  • Clear browser data after browsing.

The exact controls and terminology depend on the browser and device you use.

Blocking Cookies

Restricting cookies can affect how some websites or external services function. Essential functionality may not operate as expected if technologies required for that functionality are blocked.

10

Browser and Device Controls

Cookie management is generally available through the privacy, security or site settings within your browser.

Because browsers and devices change over time, users should consult the help information provided by their browser or device provider for current instructions.

Private or incognito browsing modes may also affect how browser information is stored, but their behaviour depends on the browser being used.

11

If the live website later introduces non-essential cookies or technologies that require user consent, appropriate cookie controls should be implemented before or when those technologies become active.

Any consent interface should clearly explain the relevant cookie categories and provide users with appropriate choices where required.

This policy should also be updated to reflect the technologies actually in use.
