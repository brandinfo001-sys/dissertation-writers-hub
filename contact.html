Before You Message

A Few Details Can Make Your Enquiry Clearer

You can keep your first message short. Where relevant, including the following information can help explain what you need.

  • 01
    Type of academic task

    Dissertation, assignment, coursework, essay, thesis, research or another university task.

  • 02
    Subject or discipline

    Briefly identify the academic area your work relates to.

  • 03
    Instructions or brief

    Mention the key requirements or explain what your university has asked you to do.

  • 04
    Current stage

    Explain whether you are planning, researching, drafting, reviewing or finalising the work.

  • 05
    Relevant timescale

    If there is an important academic deadline, mention it when explaining your requirements.

Helpful Information

What Makes a Useful Academic Enquiry?

There is no need to write a long introduction. Focus on the academic information that helps explain your project and the support you are looking for.

  • ✓
    Academic task

    Tell us whether you are working on a dissertation, assignment, research project or another task.

  • ✓
    Instructions

    Mention the important requirements from your brief or assessment criteria.

  • ✓
    Support area

    Explain whether you need help understanding structure, research, methodology, proofreading, referencing or another area.

  • ✓
    Current progress

    Briefly explain what you have already completed and where you are currently stuck.

  • ✓
    Relevant deadline

    Mention an important submission date where it is relevant to the discussion.
