Academic Support Disclaimer

Dissertation Writers Hub presents academic support across areas such as dissertations, assignments, coursework, essays, theses, research, methodology, proofreading, editing, referencing, formatting and academic consultation.

Descriptions of academic support on this website are general. The relevance and nature of any support will depend on the academic task, instructions and circumstances discussed.

Website content should not be treated as a substitute for instructions, feedback or rules issued by a university, tutor, supervisor, module leader or other academic authority.

Your Responsibility

It is your responsibility to ensure that any material you submit complies with the academic integrity and authorship rules applicable to your assessment.
