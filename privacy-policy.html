01

Introduction

Dissertation Writers Hub respects the importance of privacy and aims to handle information shared through this website in a responsible and appropriate manner.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be processed when you use the website, contact us through available communication channels or follow links to third-party platforms.

By using the website, you acknowledge that information may be handled as described in this policy and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

02

Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to information associated with your use of the Dissertation Writers Hub website and communications initiated through contact methods presented on the website.

It does not control how independent third-party websites, applications or platforms handle information once you leave this website.

Third-Party Platforms

Links to services such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Threads take you to platforms operated by third parties. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you use those services.

03

Information We May Collect

The information involved will depend on how you interact with the website and which contact method you choose to use.

Information You Choose to Provide

When contacting Dissertation Writers Hub, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Your name.
  • A telephone or WhatsApp contact number.
  • Your academic level or subject area.
  • The type of academic support you want to discuss.
  • Information about an assignment, dissertation, thesis, research project or other academic task.
  • Relevant instructions, requirements or deadlines that you choose to share.
  • Messages and other information included in your enquiry.

Website and Technical Information

Depending on the website hosting and technical services in use, certain technical information may be processed automatically. This can include information such as:

  • Browser and device information.
  • General technical information relating to website access.
  • Pages or resources requested from the website.
  • Basic server or security logs generated by the website hosting environment.

Dissertation Writers Hub should not request passwords, payment-card information or unrelated sensitive personal information through the website enquiry interface.

04

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used where reasonably necessary to operate the website, understand enquiries and communicate about academic support requirements.

This may include using relevant information to:

  • Understand and respond to an enquiry.
  • Identify the academic support area being requested.
  • Communicate with you through the contact method you choose.
  • Maintain appropriate records relating to communications.
  • Operate, maintain and protect the website.
  • Investigate technical or security issues.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations where necessary.
Current Website Enquiry Form

The website enquiry form is currently designed as a frontend interface only. Unless a form-processing backend is later configured, information entered into that interface is not represented as being sent to Dissertation Writers Hub.

05

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a lawful basis for processing personal information, the appropriate basis will depend on the circumstances and the nature of the interaction.

Relevant bases may include:

  • Consent — where you have actively agreed to a particular use of your information.
  • Steps requested by you — where processing is necessary to respond to an enquiry or take steps you have asked for before entering into an arrangement.
  • Legitimate interests — where information is reasonably needed to operate, secure or communicate through the website and those interests are not overridden by applicable privacy rights.
  • Legal obligations — where processing is necessary to comply with an applicable legal requirement.
06

Sharing Information

Personal information should not be shared with unrelated third parties without an appropriate reason.

Information may, where necessary, be handled by service providers or platforms involved in operating the website, hosting communications or providing functionality requested by the user.

Information may also need to be disclosed where required by applicable law, a valid legal process or where reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, security or the integrity of the website.

Dissertation Writers Hub does not represent personal information as being sold to third parties through this website.

07

External and Third-Party Services

The website contains links to external platforms that operate independently from Dissertation Writers Hub.

These currently include links to:

  • WhatsApp.
  • Instagram.
  • Facebook.
  • Threads.

When you follow an external link, information may be processed by the company operating that platform according to its own privacy policy, cookie practices and terms of use.

External Privacy Policies

Dissertation Writers Hub does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party platforms. You should review the relevant platform's own privacy information before providing personal information through it.

08

Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including where retention is needed for communication, record-keeping, security or applicable legal requirements.

The appropriate retention period can vary depending on the type of information, the nature of the interaction and any relevant operational or legal requirements.

Information that is no longer reasonably required should be deleted, anonymised or otherwise handled appropriately where practicable.

09

Data Security

Reasonable attention should be given to protecting information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no website, internet connection, messaging platform or electronic storage method can be represented as completely secure.

Users should therefore avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information and should take appropriate precautions when communicating online.

Please Do Not Send

Do not send passwords, banking credentials, complete payment-card details or unrelated highly sensitive personal information through website enquiries or general messaging channels.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the law applicable to your information and the circumstances of the processing, you may have rights in relation to personal information held about you.

These may include rights to:

  • Request information about how your personal information is being used.
  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion of information in circumstances where deletion is appropriate.
  • Request restriction of certain processing where applicable.
  • Object to certain uses of personal information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

Whether a particular right applies will depend on the circumstances and applicable data protection requirements.

11

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The use of cookies or similar browser technologies may depend on the website hosting, analytics and other services that are actually configured.

More information about website cookies and related technologies is provided separately in the Cookie Policy .

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, communication methods, third-party services or applicable requirements.

Where the policy is updated, the revision date shown near the top of this page should also be updated.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically if they want to understand the current version of the website's privacy information.
